# Portfolio Website Issues - Academic Transformation Needed

## Current Problems: Too Gimmicky, Not Professional

### 1. **Excessive Visual Effects** 🎨
**Problem:** The site feels more like a creative showcase than an academic portfolio

- **Particle background animation** - Distracting, doesn't serve functional purpose
- **Parallax tilt effects** on cards - Gimmicky hover animations
- **Typewriter effect** on landing page - Cute but unprofessional for academic context
- **Wave emoji** (👋) in greeting - Too casual
- **Purple color scheme** (`#c770f0`) - Feels more game-dev than academic
- **Gradient overlays** everywhere - Overdone visual styling

**Impact:** Looks like a hobbyist portfolio rather than serious academic/research profile

---

### 2. **Informal Tone & Content** 💬
**Problem:** Content is too casual for academic presentation

- **"Hi There!" greeting** - Too informal
- **Quote: "Strive to build things that make a difference!"** - Generic, not scholarly
- **About section mentions**: "Playing Games" as first hobby - Doesn't project professionalism
- **Project descriptions** are casual, lack technical depth
- **No citations, publications, or research contributions** highlighted

**Impact:** Doesn't establish credibility as researcher/academic

---

### 3. **Layout & Structure Issues** 📐
**Problem:** Design prioritizes style over substance

- **Large decorative images** take up valuable space
- **Travel blog section** - While nice, not relevant for academic portfolio
- **Projects lack depth** - Missing methodology, results, publications
- **No clear research focus** - Projects seem random, not thematically connected
- **Resume as separate page** - Should integrate key achievements throughout

**Impact:** Visitors can't quickly understand research interests and contributions

---

### 4. **Missing Academic Elements** 🎓
**Problem:** Lacks essential components of academic portfolios

Missing:
- Publications section (papers, conferences, journals)
- Research interests statement
- Academic appointments/positions
- Teaching experience
- Grants/funding
- Collaborations and lab affiliations
- CV download (not just visual resume)
- Google Scholar/ResearchGate links
- Academic email/institutional affiliation

**Impact:** Doesn't compete with standard academic CVs/portfolios

---

### 5. **Technical Presentation Issues** ⚙️
**Problem:** Projects presented as demos rather than research contributions

Issues:
- **No methodology sections** - How was it built? What's novel?
- **Missing evaluation metrics** - No performance data, benchmarks
- **No related work** - Not positioned within research context
- **GitHub links only** - Need papers, reports, demos with context
- **Image-focused cards** - Should emphasize findings over screenshots

**Impact:** Work appears as coding exercises rather than research

---

## Summary: What Needs to Change

### Remove/Minimize:
- ❌ Particles, animations, parallax effects
- ❌ Typewriter, wave emojis, casual greetings
- ❌ Purple gradients and flashy colors
- ❌ Travel blog (or move to separate personal site)
- ❌ Generic motivational quotes

### Add/Emphasize:
- ✅ Publications list with proper citations
- ✅ Research statement and interests
- ✅ Technical depth in project descriptions
- ✅ Academic credentials prominently
- ✅ Clean, readable typography
- ✅ Proper sectioning (Research, Teaching, Service)
- ✅ Professional color scheme (navy, gray, white)
- ✅ Data visualizations of results

### Design Direction:
**From:** Colorful, animated, game-developer portfolio  
**To:** Clean, professional, research-focused academic CV

Think: **Stanford/MIT faculty pages**, not creative agency showcase

---

**Priority:** High - Current design may hurt applications/opportunities  
**Timeline:** Incremental redesign recommended  
**References:** Look at portfolios of successful PhD candidates and junior faculty in CS/AI
