I build systems that don't just work, but scale with integrity.

My work lives at the intersection of deep academic rigor and practical software engineering. As an AI/ML Application Developer & Researcher, I focus on constructing architectures that respect the complexities of machine learning models while delivering robust, scalable solutions for real-world applications.

I view code not just as instructions for a machine, but as a formal expression of logic and intent. This philosophy drives me to create minimalist, architecturally sound systems where every component serves a distinct, measurable purpose.

Core Philosophy

Reliability by Design

Ensuring measurable quality in agentic workflows. Every system is built with fault tolerance, predictable execution paths, and rigorous logging, treating reliability as a primary feature rather than an afterthought.

The Human Element

Focusing on AI as an extension of human capability. I design interfaces and model interactions that augment human reasoning, prioritizing transparency and user agency in complex decision-making processes.

Academic Foundations

Maintaining the rigor of research in production code. I actively translate complex academic papers into scalable, optimized production systems, ensuring theoretical advancements are grounded in practical reality.

Research & Influence

Large Language Models

Exploring the boundaries of context window utilization and efficient parameter fine-tuning. This shapes my approach to building retrieval-augmented generation systems that are both accurate and resource-efficient.

Medical Deep Learning

Applying computer vision and sequence modeling to complex healthcare datasets. The strict regulatory and accuracy requirements in this field inform my uncompromising stance on model interpretability and validation.

Agentic Workflows

Designing autonomous systems capable of multi-step reasoning and tool use. My work focuses on creating robust frameworks that prevent hallucination and ensure deterministic outcomes in non-deterministic environments.

Architectural Interests

Offline Pursuits

The principles of digital architecture are often reflections of the physical world. When not designing software systems, I study brutalist architecture, mid-century typography, and horology. These disciplines—where form strictly follows function and durability is paramount—heavily influence my approach to creating clean, enduring codebases.
